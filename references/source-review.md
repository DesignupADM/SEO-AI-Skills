## Claims not promoted to operating rules

- Meta keywords: Google says this tag does not affect indexing or ranking. Do not add it as Google SEO work. [Google supported metadata](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags)
- Routine disavowal: most sites do not need it. Investigate relevant link-spam/manual-action evidence before recommending specialist work; disavowal is not a duplicate-content removal mechanism. [Search Console disavow guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/2648487)
- URL Parameters tool instructions: the tool was deprecated in 2022; do not include its old interface in a current task. [Google retirement notice](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/03/url-parameters-tool-deprecated)
- Legacy AMP, cache-search, preferred-domain, and old analytics instructions in S5 are not accepted current procedures. Consult current product documentation if a task needs these capabilities.
- Fixed character counts, word counts, keyword placements, single-H1 prescriptions, and update cadences are editorial conventions, not universal search eligibility tests.
- Do not automatically nofollow every external link, consolidate similar products, retire older content, or buy dedicated hosting on the basis of these articles. Establish the actual problem first.
- General claims about traffic, dwell time, freshness, brand mentions, or E-E-A-T do not establish a measurable ranking factor or causal AI citation benefit for the reviewed page.

The first three corrections were checked against official documentation during import. Other excluded prescriptions are treated as unverified or context-dependent rather than silently replaced with new platform claims. Verify current requirements when a task depends on them.
